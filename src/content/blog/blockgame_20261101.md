I made Blockgame for an assignment to create a game similar to the classic Dino runner. The goal is to keep running for as long as possible while avoiding obstacles. I gave the player a square character, inspired by Geometry Dash, and replaced the cacti with spikes.

I added a coin system, too: passing a spike earns a coin. You can spend coins in the shop to unlock character colors, accessories, and visual effects. There is also a jackpot spinner where you can try your luck for a chance to win more coins.

The game also changes between day and night and includes a rocket booster to help you fly over obstacles. Your coins and purchased items are saved in your browser, so progress stays there when you return using the same browser.
